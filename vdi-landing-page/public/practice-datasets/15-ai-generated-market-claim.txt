The global last-mile delivery market reached $168 billion in 2024 and is growing at 11.4% CAGR through 2030. Same-day delivery now accounts for 29% of urban parcels in North America. Failed first attempts cost carriers $21 per parcel on average, and retailers that verify address quality before dispatch cut those failures by half. Two regional carriers quietly raised surcharges 18% in Q1 2026, which analysts say will push mid-market merchants toward consolidators.
